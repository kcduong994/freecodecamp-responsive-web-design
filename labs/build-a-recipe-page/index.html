Fish Recipe

Make a simple pan-fried fish dish with lemon, salt, and black pepper.

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless fish fillets (about 150 g each)
  • 1 tablespoon of cooking oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon of black pepper
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges

Instructions

  1. Pat the fish fillets dry with a clean paper towel.
  2. Season both sides of the fish with salt and black pepper.
  3. Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
  4. Cook the fillets for about 3–4 minutes on each side, adjusting for thickness.
  5. Check that the fish reaches 63°C (145°F) in the thickest part, then serve with lemon wedges.
